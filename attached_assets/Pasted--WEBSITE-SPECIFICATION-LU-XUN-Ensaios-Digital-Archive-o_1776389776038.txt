# WEBSITE SPECIFICATION: "LU XUN - Ensaios (杂文)" 
## Digital Archive of Critical Essays & Prose Poetry (1918-1936)

---

## 1. CONTENT ARCHITECTURE: THE 16 COLLECTIONS

Structure the database around Lu Xun's 16 essay collections (*zawen* 杂文), organized chronologically by publication date [^15^][^16^][^17^]. Each collection has a distinct political "flavor" that should be visually indicated.

### Collection Database Schema

| Collection (CN) | Collection (PT) | Year | Volume | Essay Count | Characteristics |
|-----------------|-----------------|------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| 热风 | **Vento Quente** (Hot Wind) | 1925 | Vol. 2 | 41 | Early social critique, iconoclasm |
| 坟 | **A Tumba** (The Grave) | 1927 | Vol. 2 | 23 | Academic essays, literature/philo |
| 华盖集 | **O Galho da Caça** (Galloping Gables/Bad Luck) | 1926 | Vol. 3 | 31 | Debates with rivals, polemics |
| 华盖集续编 | **O Galho da Caça (Continuação)** | 1927 | Vol. 3 | 26 | Post-3.18 Massacre, anger |
| 而已集 | **E Só Isso** (And That's All/Only That) | 1928 | Vol. 3 | 30 | Post-1927 political purge |
| 三闲集 | **Três Ocios** (Three Leisures) | 1932 | Vol. 4 | 34 | Marxist criticism, creationism |
| 二心集 | **Dois Corações** (Two Hearts) | 1932 | Vol. 4 | 37 | "Bias towards the proletariat" |
| 南腔北调集 | **Sotaques do Norte e Sul** (Mixed Accents) | 1934 | Vol. 4 | 50 | Defense against attacks, irony |
| 伪自由书 | **Falsa Liberdade** (False Freedom) | 1933 | Vol. 5 | 43 | Shanghai press censorship era |
| 准风月谈 | **Quase-Fengyue** (Quasi-Amorous Talks) | 1934 | Vol. 5 | 64 | "Pseudo-classical" style |
| 花边文学 | **Literatura de Renda** (Lace Literature) | 1936 | Vol. 5 | 61 | Short press pieces, "decorative" |
| 且介亭杂文 | **Ensaios do Pavilhão Qiejie** (Essays from Qiejie Pavilion) | 1935 | Vol. 6 | 48 | Mature late style, Shanghai isolation |
| 且介亭杂文二集 | **Ensaios do Pavilhão Qiejie (Segundo Vol.)** | 1935 | Vol. 6 | 75 | (Approximate) |
| 且介亭杂文末编 | **Ensaios do Pavilhão Qiejie (Final)** | 1936 | Vol. 7 | 35 | Deathbed writings, incomplete |
| 集外集 | **Fora do Cânon** (Beyond the Canon) | 1936+ | Vol. 7 | Variable | Uncollected early pieces |
| 集外集拾遗 | **Suplemento Fora do Cânon** | 1936+ | Vol. 7 | Variable | Posthumous discoveries |

**Plus**: 
- **野草 (Erva Selvagem)**: *Wild Grass* (1927) - 23 prose poems (散文诗). Treat as separate "Collection 17" with poetic formatting [^20^].
